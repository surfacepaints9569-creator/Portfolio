About Me

Turning business operations into smart systems

Praveen Yadav is an AI Automation Engineer and MIS Executive with a systems-builder's instinct for turning repetitive business processes into intelligent, self-running software. Since 2022, he has driven automation, ERP development, and business intelligence at Surface Paints Pvt. Ltd., where he designs and ships internal tools — from costing engines to live sales dashboards — that departments rely on daily. Alongside that role, he also builds and maintains internal web applications for Link HR Solution Pvt. Ltd.

His work spans the full stack of business technology: Google Apps Script automation, custom ERP and CRM platforms, Power BI and MIS dashboards, and clean, responsive web applications. He's equally comfortable modelling a formulation-based costing engine, wiring up a role-based CRM, or designing the brochure that goes out to a client.

Praveen is now expanding into freelance ERP development — helping manufacturing and service businesses replace spreadsheets and manual reporting with secure, scalable systems built on real databases and proper access control.
